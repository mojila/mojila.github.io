03Why hold it at all

  • It is rupiah spending money. The equity sleeve pays rupiah dividends but is fully invested; the money market balance absorbs rupiah costs without forcing a sale of BBRI or the other IDX names.
  • It is the least volatile rupiah asset in the book. Money market funds aim at stable NAV with same-day or next-day redemption, which is the closest local substitute for a rupiah savings balance.
  • It is small enough to be optional. At roughly *** of external IDR assets and *** of the combined book by translation, it neither drives nor rescues total return.

04What this sleeve does not do

Money market funds accrue return through NAV rather than paying distributions, so these balances are excluded from the dividend forecast on the planner. They are also not a yield play by the standard used here: Indonesian money market funds have historically run below the *** USD cash hurdle, and no current rate was captured for either fund. Treat the sleeve as liquidity, not as income.

The open question is the Bahana balance. If it is an emergency buffer, *** is reasonable and needs no action. If it is idle cash waiting for a decision, it is competing with a *** dollar alternative — and the currency mismatch, not the fund, is the real choice.
