The cash-plus book

Why an overwhelming cash position is the portfolio's engine rather than a failure to invest, what the 3.38% hurdle rate implies for every dollar moved into risk, and the four flags that make the equity sleeve look like a hobby allocation.

Read the note →

Principles

Hurdle
3.38% is the bar. Cash pays it without drawdown; any risk asset must beat it net of fees and tax to have been worth the move.
Structure
Capital preservation first, with a small growth kicker. Judge the book against that stance, not against a growth benchmark it was never sized for.
Friction
Small tickets cost roughly 0.3–0.4% all-in. Batch purchases; a ticket can spend a month of the money's yield before it earns anything.
